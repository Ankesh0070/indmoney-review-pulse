# Sample Q&A — INDmoney MF FAQ Assistant

These are real outputs from the prototype (v2, with bug fixes). Every factual answer carries one official source and a "Last updated from sources: 23 Jun 2026" stamp (omitted below for brevity, shown in the UI).

---

**1. Q: "What is the exit load of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?"**
A: HDFC Flexi Cap Fund charges an exit load of 1% if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year of allotment, and Nil after 1 year. No entry load applies (entry loads are not permitted in India).
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct

**2. Q: "What is the ELSS lock-in period?"**
A: HDFC ELSS Tax Saver has a statutory lock-in of 3 years from the date of allotment; each SIP instalment is locked for 3 years from its own date. Investments are eligible for deduction up to ₹1.5 lakh under Section 80C (old tax regime).
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver/direct

**3. Q: "Minimum SIP of HDFC ELSS Tax Saver?"**
A: The minimum SIP / application amount for HDFC ELSS Tax Saver is ₹500 (additional purchases are also ₹500).
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver/direct

**4. Q: "What is the benchmark of HDFC Mid Cap Fund?"**
A: HDFC Mid Cap Fund (formerly HDFC Mid-Cap Opportunities Fund) is benchmarked against the NIFTY Midcap 150 TRI.
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-mid-cap-fund/direct

**5. Q: "Riskometer of HDFC Large Cap Fund?"**
A: HDFC Large Cap Fund (formerly HDFC Top 100 Fund) is classified as "Very High" risk on its product riskometer (per SEBI's product-labelling norms). The live riskometer image is shown on the official scheme page.
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund/direct

**6. Q: "What is the expense ratio of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?"**
A: The Total Expense Ratio (TER) of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund changes monthly and is lower for the Direct plan than the Regular plan, so the live figure is published on the official scheme page and the monthly factsheet. Please open the official page below for the current TER.
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct

**7. Q: "How do I download my capital gains statement?"**
A: HDFC Mutual Fund is serviced by the registrar CAMS, so you can request a Capital Gain/Loss Statement (realised gains on a FIFO basis, split into short-term and long-term) from the official CAMS page. It is emailed to the address registered in your folio — you don't enter any PAN or details here.
🔗 Source: https://www.camsonline.com/Investors/Statements/Capital-Gain&Capital-Loss-statement

**8. Q: "Does HDFC Large Cap Fund charge entry load?"** *(new in v2)*
A: Entry loads are not permitted in India (SEBI banned them in 2009). HDFC Large Cap Fund (formerly HDFC Top 100 Fund) does not charge any entry load.
🔗 Source: https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-cap-fund/direct

🔗 Source: https://investor.sebi.gov.in/
